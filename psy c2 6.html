Perceptual Constancy and Illusion

Size, Shape & Brightness Constancy, Optical Illusions (Sphinx, Moon, Muller-Lyer), and ESP

1 Perceptual Constancy (Size, Shape & Brightness)

As a friend walks away, their retinal image shrinks—yet you do not think they are physically shrinking! Perceptual constancy is the brain's tendency to perceive objects as stable in size, shape, and brightness even as retinal images constantly change.

A. Size Constancy

Perceiving an object as maintaining its true size regardless of distance (two identical cars half a block vs. one block away still look the same real size). Warning: Alcohol disrupts size constancy, causing young adults to underestimate object sizes and increasing accident risk.

B. Shape Constancy

Perceiving an object's true shape across viewing angles (a tilted rectangular book creates a slanted retinal image yet is still seen as rectangular).

C. Brightness Constancy

A white shirt still looks white in bright sunlight and dim rooms (though brightness constancy depends on surrounding objects—alone in dim light it looks duller).

2 Perceptual Illusions (When Perception Misleads)

A perceptual illusion occurs when our perception differs from actual physical reality—revealing how normal perceptual cues can misfire:

A. The Sphinx Illusion (Edgar Allan Poe)

A man looking out a window perceives a small insect on the glass as a giant monster on a distant mountain because he misapplies distance and size constancy cues.

B. Moon Illusion

The moon appears larger near the horizon than high overhead, even though its retinal image size is essentially identical in both positions.

C. Muller-Lyer Illusion

Two physically equal lines appear unequal because arrow ends resemble inside room corners (interpreted as farther/longer) vs. outside building corners.

3 Extra-Sensory Perception (ESP) & Summary Comparisons

Extra-Sensory Perception (ESP):

Normally we perceive via five senses (seeing, hearing, touching, tasting, smelling). ESP is the claimed paranormal ability to know about people, objects, or events without any sensory contact (e.g., mind reading). Scientific Status: ESP is a proposed paranormal phenomenon, not an established scientific ability—claims lack reliable, reproducible scientific evidence.
