Operations on Complex Numbers

Component Addition, Subtraction, FOIL Multiplication, and Conjugate Division

Standard Operational Form:

Let two arbitrary complex numbers be expressed in canonical rectangular form:

$$z_1 = a + ib \quad \text{and} \quad z_2 = c + id \quad (a, b, c, d \in \mathbb{R})$$

1 Addition of Complex Numbers

The Component-Wise Addition Law:

Combine real parts together and imaginary parts together:

$$\boxed{z_1 + z_2 = (a + c) + i(b + d)}$$
Example A: Direct Combination

Evaluate \((2 + 3i) + (4 + 5i)\):

\(= (2 + 4) + (3 + 5)i\)
\(= \mathbf{6 + 8i}\)
Example B: Involving Negative Coefficients

Evaluate \((5 - 2i) + (3 + 7i)\):

\(= (5 + 3) + (-2 + 7)i\)
\(= \mathbf{8 + 5i}\)

2 Subtraction of Complex Numbers

The Subtraction Law:

Subtract the corresponding real and imaginary parts:

$$\boxed{z_1 - z_2 = (a - c) + i(b - d)}$$
Example A: Standard Subtraction

Evaluate \((7 + 6i) - (3 + 2i)\):

\(= (7 - 3) + (6 - 2)i\)
\(= \mathbf{4 + 4i}\)
Example B: Sign Distribution Trap

Evaluate \((5 + 2i) - (3 - 4i)\):

Distribute the negative sign: \(= 5 + 2i - 3 + 4i\)
Group terms: \(= (5 - 3) + (2 + 4)i\)
\(= \mathbf{2 + 6i}\)

3 Multiplication of Complex Numbers

Multiplication uses ordinary algebraic polynomial binomial expansion (FOIL: First, Outer, Inner, Last), governed by the fundamental property \(i^2 = -1\).

General Formula Derivation:
\((a + ib)(c + id) = ac + i(ad) + i(bc) + i^2(bd)\)
Substitute \(i^2 = -1\): \(= ac + i(ad + bc) - bd\)
$$\boxed{z_1 \cdot z_2 = (ac - bd) + i(ad + bc)}$$
Step-by-Step Multiplication: \((2 + 3i)(4 + i)\)
FOIL expansion: \(= 2(4) + 2(i) + 3i(4) + 3i(i)\)
\(= 8 + 2i + 12i + 3i^2\)
Apply \(i^2 = -1\): \(= 8 + 14i + 3(-1) = 8 + 14i - 3\)
\(= \mathbf{5 + 14i}\)
The Essential Substitution Rule: Whenever \(i^2\) appears, immediately replace it with \(-1\). For instance, \(4i^2 = 4(-1) = -4\) and \(7i^2 = -7\). Never leave an \(i^2\) term in a final canonical complex form!

4 Division & The Complex Conjugate Technique

Direct division by an imaginary number is undefined. To express a quotient in standard form \(x + iy\), we multiply both numerator and denominator by the complex conjugate of the denominator, transforming the denominator into a strictly positive real number.

Conjugate Realization Property:

The complex conjugate of \(c + id\) is \(c - id\). Their product is always real:

$$(c + id)(c - id) = c^2 - (id)^2 = c^2 - i^2 d^2 = c^2 + d^2 \in \mathbb{R}$$
Worked Division: Evaluate \(\frac{2 + 3i}{4 + i}\)
Step 1: Multiply numerator & denominator by conjugate \((4 - i)\):
\(=\frac{(2 + 3i)(4 - i)}{(4 + i)(4 - i)}\)
Step 2: Expand numerator and denominator:
\(= \frac{8 - 2i + 12i - 3i^2}{16 - i^2} = \frac{8 + 10i - 3(-1)}{16 - (-1)}\)
Step 3: Simplify real terms:
\(= \frac{8 + 10i + 3}{16 + 1} = \frac{11 + 10i}{17}\)
Standard Form: \(= \mathbf{\frac{11}{17} + \frac{10}{17}i}\)

5 Operational Summary: Benchmark Case \((z_1 = 2+3i, \; z_2 = 4+i)\)

Operation Operational Rule Computed Result
Addition Add corresponding components \(6 + 4i\)
Subtraction Subtract corresponding components \(-2 + 2i\)
Multiplication FOIL expansion with \(i^2 = -1\) \(5 + 14i\)
Division Multiply by denominator's conjugate \(\frac{11}{17} + \frac{10}{17}i\)
