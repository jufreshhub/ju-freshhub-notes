Paleozoic Era (600–225 million years ago)

Denudation of Ancient Mountains, Peneplain Surfaces, Inselbergs, and Sediment Transport

1 What Was the Paleozoic Era?

Extending from 600–225 million years ago, the Paleozoic Era followed the Precambrian. While the name means ancient life, in Ethiopian geology the era is dominated by physical landscape transformations:

  • Denudation of ancient mountains
  • Formation of peneplained surfaces
  • Development of inselbergs
  • Transportation and deposition of sediments

Analogy: Over millions of years, rain and rivers wear down a rock mountain until large parts become relatively flat while resistant rock masses remain standing.

2 Denudation of Ancient Mountains

During the Paleozoic, ancient mountains built by earlier tectonic movements underwent prolonged denudation (the general wearing away and lowering of the Earth's surface). Weathering broke down exposed rocks, and rivers removed debris—much like water dissolving and washing away parts of a tall pile of sugar cubes.

3 Formation of Peneplained Surfaces

A peneplain is a relatively low-relief surface produced through prolonged erosion and denudation (not necessarily perfectly flat, as hills and resistant rocks can remain).

Stage 1: High Relief

High mountains and deep valleys.

Stage 2: Denudation

Erosion gradually lowers elevation.

Stage 3: Peneplain

Landscape becomes low and smooth.

4 Inselbergs & Sediment Transport

Inselbergs (“Island Mountains”):

Isolated hills rising abruptly above a flat plain because harder, resistant rock survives while softer surrounding rock erodes away.

Transportation & Deposition:

Running water, wind, and gravity carry fragments downstream, depositing them when speed decreases: Erosion → Transportation → Deposition.

5 Why Are Paleozoic Rocks Rare in Ethiopia?

Paleozoic rock formations are poorly represented in Ethiopia because prolonged denudation stripped away massive quantities of material from older surfaces while extensive sedimentary deposition and preservation during this era remained limited.
